Every Heartbeat Analyzed. Every Anomaly Caught.

The Challenge: Traditional ECG monitoring relies on intermittent snapshots or delayed analysis. Critical arrhythmias like atrial fibrillation can develop silently between recordings — putting patients at risk of stroke and heart failure.

The CardioStream Solution: Our AI continuously ingests live ECG data streams from wearable medical patches, filtering out motion artifacts and electrical noise to deliver clean, analyzable signals. Deep learning models detect atrial fibrillation, ventricular tachycardia, and other arrhythmias with 99.4% accuracy — alerting cardiologists in under 2 seconds.
